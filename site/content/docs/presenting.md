## The presenter view

{{< shot name="presenter" caption="The presenter view: thumbnails, the slide's frontmatter, the slide, and its notes." >}}

The presenter view (⌘2) shows the deck as you will present it:

- **Thumbnails** of every slide on the left. Click one to go to it, or use
  the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Space, Home and End.
- The slide's **frontmatter** above it: its number, title and file, and
  chips for its template, layout and theme. A dashed chip is a default,
  not set in the slide's frontmatter.
- The **slide**, large.
- The slide's **speaker notes** below it: everything after its `!--` line.
  ⇧⌘O hides or shows them, and the bar above them resizes them.

Back in the editor (⌘1), the slide you were on is open.

## The slideshow

**▶ Present** in the toolbar (⌘↩) shows the deck full screen from the
slide you are on, and ⇧⌘↩ starts from the first slide.

{{< shot name="slideshow" caption="The slideshow fills the screen with the slide." >}}

| To | Press |
|---|---|
| Go to the next slide | →, ↓, Space, Return, Page Down, or click |
| Go back a slide | ←, ↑, Page Up, or click the left third of the screen |
| Go to the first or last slide | Home or End |
| End the show | Esc |

The slide's number shows for a moment after each move, and the pointer hides
when you stop moving it. When the show ends, InkStudio is on the slide you
reached.
